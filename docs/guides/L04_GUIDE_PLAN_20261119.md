## Reader and thesis
Audience: CTO, platform engineering, AI operations and AppSec reviewers connecting agents to MCP, CRM, mail and repository tools.
Question: How can an operator verify that an approved action was executed once, by the correct identity, on the correct object, and that the effect is independently observed?
Thesis: The model proposes; a separate authority service enforces; the destination and evidence system confirm.

## Outline
1. Authority chain: actor, agent, MCP, downstream API, object and receipt; explain HTTP MCP OAuth audience binding and qualification of STDIO.
2. Permission matrix: read, draft, externally visible writes, release actions, destructive actions; policy defaults are proposals, not universal risk rankings.
3. Human approval: exact payload preview, owner, expiry, version, single consumption, recheck at the executor.
4. Audit trail: correlation IDs, redacted arguments, approval events, receipt and independently observed target state.
5. Five synthetic negative tests: unapproved write, changed object, replayed approval, wrong audience, timeout/reconciliation.
6. Decision memo and limitations: PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN bounded to a tested path, false-green avoidance, retry safety.
7. Source list and explicit non-guarantee boundary.
